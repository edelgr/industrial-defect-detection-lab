**RGB** — reflected light: surface appearance (scratches, stains, colour).
**Infrared (lock-in thermography)** — how heat from modulated excitation diffuses: reveals
*internal* flaws. The displayed image is a normalised visualisation, not a calibrated temperature.
**3D scan** — geometry in physical units: dents, bulges, missing material.
A defect may be visible in only one of the three — compare the per-modality labels above.
