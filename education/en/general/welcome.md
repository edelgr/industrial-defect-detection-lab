## Welcome to IDD Lab

**Industrial Defect Detection Lab** is an interactive laboratory for learning how industrial
defects and anomalies are detected — from classical image processing to foundation models and
multimodal 3D inspection.

What makes it a *laboratory* rather than a slide deck:

- **Real datasets.** Browse normal and defective samples of research benchmarks you install
  locally (IDD Lab never redistributes data).
- **Real implementations.** Every runnable method executes its actual implementation (OpenCV,
  scikit-image, Anomalib, Open3D) on your GPU or CPU.
- **Traceable science.** Every dataset and method shows its original paper, PDF, authors' code,
  the implementation actually used, licences and BibTeX.
- **Honest evaluation.** Thresholds are calibrated on normal images only; results are labelled
  *local educational* or *synthetic demonstration* and are never mixed with official benchmark
  numbers.

### Where to start
1. **Dataset Research Atlas** — why so many benchmarks exist and how they differ.
2. **Dataset Explorer** — look at good and defective parts and their ground truth.
3. **Methods Library** — how each family works, step by step.
4. **Methods Laboratory** and **Compare Methods** — run methods and measure them.
5. **3D & Multimodal Lab** — point clouds, infrared and fusion.
6. **Guided Workshop** — the 3-hour CIARP 2026 session.

> Raw anomaly scores are method-specific numbers, **not probabilities**. IDD Lab never shows them as
> confidence percentages.
